SQE2 Practice Tools

Free companion tools for the Uprising Test Prep SQE2 Study Guide

SQE2 has no multiple choice questions. It is 16 practical assessments: four oral over two half days and twelve written over three half days, each graded A to F by a trained assessor on skills and on application of law, weighted equally. The station timer below is the tool that matches the real assessment. The question practice is revision on the underlying law, and nothing more.
📚

Digital Station Library

The other 45 practice stations, 35 written and 10 oral, free and only here: the real partner email and file for every station, a real countdown timer, and a link straight to its model answer. Access code required.
⏱

Station Timer

Runs the real clocks: 10, 25 and 25 minutes for the client interview and handwritten attendance note, 45 and 15 for advocacy, and 60, 60, 45 and 30 for the four written skills. Grade yourself A to F against the genuine criteria. Access code required.
✎

Question Practice

Supplementary questions on the underlying law behind the five practice areas. Revision self-testing only, not a model of the assessment. Access code required.
📚

Digital Flashcards

500 flip cards covering all six legal skills, all five practice areas, and the ethics that runs through every station.
✅

Model Answers

Model answers, full marking grids and the hidden conduct issue for all 90 practice stations, the 45 printed in the book and the 45 in the digital station library. No access code, scan and read.
